How it works

From application to bench.

01

Apply

A short form: who you are, what you want to build, and your experience level. About two minutes.

02

Meet & tour

Our lab manager reaches out to talk through your project and walk you through the space. Reviewed on a rolling basis.

03

Sign & set up

Sign the membership agreement, pick your tier, and start your recurring membership.

04

Safety onboarding

Complete BSL-1 orientation and get your project approved. Quick, required, and what keeps the lab open.

05

Get your badge

Access, equipment booking, and the community channel. You are in. Go build.

Safety First

Punk, not reckless.

Open access only works because the space is safe. Biopunk Lab operates at Biosafety Level 1, and every member is onboarded before touching a bench.

Read the biosafety handbook →
  • 01 Every member project is reviewed and approved before work begins.
  • 02 Posted safety procedures, PPE, and waste handling at every bench.
  • 03 New materials go through a simple approval step against our inventory.
  • 04 We follow the community-bio code of ethics. No BSL-2 or higher work.
